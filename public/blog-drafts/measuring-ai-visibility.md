# Measuring AI Visibility: Beyond Google Analytics

**Phase 4, Article 17**

## Key Takeaways
- **Google Analytics cannot measure AI citations.** AI platform traffic appears as "Direct" or "Unknown Referrer" — it is invisible to traditional analytics tools.
- **Three metrics matter for AI visibility:** citation frequency (how often you are cited), citation share (your percentage of category citations), and citation sentiment (whether the citation positions your brand as the solution).
- **Citation half-life measures content durability** — how long a specific page continues to generate citations before the model shifts to newer content.
- **Competitive citation share is the North Star metric** — your brand's citations as a percentage of total citations in your category, tracked over time.

## The Measurement Gap
Traditional web analytics measure visits, time on page, conversions. AI citations produce none of these. A page cited by ChatGPT for 50 queries generates zero visits in Google Analytics. Brands optimizing for AI visibility need new measurement frameworks.

## Three Core Metrics
**Citation frequency** — raw number of times your brand is cited across LLMs per period. **Citation share** — your citations divided by total citations in your category. **Citation trend** — whether the frequency is increasing, flat, or declining. Citation share is the most strategic metric because it accounts for category growth.
