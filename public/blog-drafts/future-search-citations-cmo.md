# The Future of Search Is Citation — What CMOs Should Know for 2027

**Phase 4, Article 20**

## Key Takeaways
- **By 2027, an estimated 50%+ of all searches will return AI-generated answers** rather than blue links. Citations will be the primary unit of search visibility.
- **The brand that gets cited is the brand that gets chosen** — in a generative search world, the purchase decision is made at the citation layer, not at the search results page.
- **Entity authority will replace domain authority** — the question shifts from "which website is most trusted" to "which entity is most referenced."
- **CMOs should reallocate budget from traditional link-building to entity-building** — Wikipedia, Wikidata, schema markup, comparison content, and structured data are higher-ROI investments than guest posting and link acquisition.
- **The brands that invest in GEO in 2026 will have an insurmountable citation advantage by 2028** — entity graphs compound over time.

## The Citation Economy
The shift from links to citations as the primary unit of search value is as significant as the shift from directories to PageRank. In the citation economy, the brands that define their entities clearly, structure their content for extractability, and build durable entity associations will capture the majority of generative search traffic.

## What CMOs Should Do in 2026
1. Commission a generative web audit (Article 19) as a baseline.
2. Establish entity clarity as a core brand initiative — Wikipedia, Wikidata, schema.
3. Build the comparison page portfolio (Article 8) — this is the highest-ROI content investment.
4. Configure crawl-no-training for all AI crawlers (Article 7).
5. Shift budget from link-building to entity-building.
6. Track citation share as a board-level metric alongside organic traffic.
