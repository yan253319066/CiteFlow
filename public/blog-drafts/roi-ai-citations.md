# The ROI of AI Citations: Converting Visibility into Revenue

**Phase 4, Article 18**

## Key Takeaways
- **AI citations drive three forms of value:** direct referral traffic (clicks from AI platforms), brand awareness (impressions in AI-generated answers), and entity reinforcement (strengthened entity graph).
- **The conversion funnel for AI citations is shallower than for Google clicks** — users arriving from AI citations are further along in the buying journey because the model has partially qualified them.
- **Enterprise deals cite the vendor in 42% of procurement-related AI queries** — being cited in the evaluation phase correlates with higher win rates.
- **Attribution is possible through citation-linked UTM parameters in cited URLs** — every citeable page should include tracking parameters.

## The Three Value Layers
**Direct traffic:** Users click the citation link and land on your page. Trackable with UTM parameters on cited URLs. **Brand awareness:** The citation appears in the AI answer. The user does not click but remembers the brand name. Entity reinforcement: Every citation strengthens the model's entity graph, making future citations more likely.
