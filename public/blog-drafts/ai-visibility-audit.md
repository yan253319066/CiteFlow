# The Audit Every Brand Needs Before the Generative Web Arrives

**Phase 4, Article 19**

## Key Takeaways
- **The generative web audit covers 6 dimensions** that are not included in standard SEO audits: entity clarity, schema completeness, crawl-no-training configuration, content structure (Article 9's four layers), comparison content coverage, and agent readiness.
- **Most brands score below 50/100 on their first audit.** The most common gaps: missing Organization schema with @id, no llms.txt file, weak entity definition on subpages, and zero comparison content.
- **The audit should be conducted quarterly** because both the AI platform landscape and the brand's content inventory change faster than traditional SEO factors.

## The Six Dimensions
1. **Entity clarity** — Is your brand resolvable as an entity? Does every page include Organization schema with Wikidata @id? Is the entity definition (Layer 1) present in the first 100 words?
2. **Schema completeness** — Are Organization, Article, Product, FAQ, and BreadcrumbList schemas present where applicable?
3. **Crawler configuration** — Is robots.txt configured for crawl-no-training? Is llms.txt present and maintained?
4. **Content structure** — Do key pages satisfy all four layers (Entity Definition → Relationship Map → Proof Layer → Structured Data)?
5. **Comparison coverage** — Do you have comparison pages against market leaders, direct competitors, and alternatives?
6. **Agent readiness** — Are pricing, availability, and product specifications marked up in machine-readable schema?
